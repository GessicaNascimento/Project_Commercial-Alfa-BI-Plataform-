# Analytical Insights Report - Cosmetics Operation

## 1. Revenue Concentration by Sales Force
* **Volume vs. Revenue:** Initial analysis of the 374 data rows indicates that the ecosystem features a high transaction density per sales representative, with low granularity regarding products per order, reflecting bulk sales in sealed boxes.
* **Dynamic Unit Margin:** Because the unit price fluctuates per commercial batch reflected in the revenue calculation (`Amount ($) / Boxes Shipped`), there are clear indications of progressive volume discounts.

## 2. Geographical Patterns (Country Performance)
* The dimensional distribution of clients linked to specific countries allows for tracking whether sales expansion behaves linearly within consolidated markets or exhibits sharp fluctuations driven by the logistical seasonality of box exports (`Boxes Shipped`).
