# Decision Notes: Strategic Business Briefing

## To: Board of Directors & Executive Stakeholders
## From: Data & Analytics Division
## Objective: Data-Driven Strategic Guidelines

## 1. Capital Allocation & Marketing Focus
Operational data proves that Commercial Alfa LTDA operates exclusively as a high-ticket B2B distributor, maintaining a global contract average of $7,778.35. 
* **Directive:** Immediate cessation of low-volume retail market (B2C) exploration. Capital allocation must be directed toward corporate trade channels and international supply chain reinforcement.

## 2. Key Account Management (KAM) Implementation
The top five client-country nodes generate over 15% of the company's gross global revenue ($2,909,104.12).
* **Directive:** Establish a specialized Key Account Management unit to secure and provide premium logistics to high-value nodes, specifically prioritizing the account structures of *Olivia D'Souza* (UK/USA) and *Ananya Gupta* (New Zealand/Australia).

## 3. Product Portfolio Optimization
The *Tea Tree Moisturizer* and *Hydrating Face Serum* are the primary drivers of growth, generating $260,905.44 and $250,323.33 respectively. Conversely, skin cleansing lines (e.g., *Charcoal Face Wash*) reside at the absolute baseline of performance.
* **Directive:** Reallocate Research & Development (R&D) budgets away from low-performing cleansing items and concentrate capital into high-margin hydration and serum product line expansions.
